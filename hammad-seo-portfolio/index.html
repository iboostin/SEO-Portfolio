Ask on WhatsApp

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see early movement within two to three months, with stronger gains building over six months and beyond. Technical fixes and title improvements often show up first.

Do you guarantee first position on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking, because Google controls the results. What I can promise is a clear plan, solid execution and transparent monthly reporting on clicks, CTR and conversions.

What is included in the free SEO audit?

A review of your site's technical health, on-page basics (titles, headings, schema), content gaps and quick wins, with a short list of the fixes that will make the biggest difference.

Do you work with Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. Two of the case studies here are e-commerce: Equine Care on Shopify and Games Gravity on WooCommerce.

Can you help my site appear in AI Overviews and ChatGPT?

Yes. I structure content with clear answers, FAQ and HowTo schema and strong entity signals, which helps pages get cited in Google AI Overviews and AI assistants.

How do you report progress?

Every month you get a report from Google Search Console and analytics: clicks, impressions, CTR, average position and the pages and queries that moved, plus the plan for the next month.
