The outcome

Monthly clicks rose by about 59% compared with the earlier six-month window, and CTR improved from 0.7% to 0.9%. Daily clicks peaked at almost 150 in late August.

An average position of 10.1 keeps a wide keyword footprint at the edge of page one, which is the base for the next phase: CTR optimisation and featured snippet targeting.

Strategy applied

  • Built topical authority around GIS mapping, remote sensing, climate risk, ESG and geospatial AI, positioning the site as a reference source for these searches
  • Pillar-and-cluster content architecture: core service pages (GIS solutions, predictive modelling, climate risk adaptation, decision support and digital twins) supported by case studies, blog, news and podcast content
  • FAQ and HowTo schema markup to support rich results and Google AI Overview eligibility
  • Ongoing competitor gap analysis against geospatial and climate-tech firms
  • Technical health: Core Web Vitals, crawl optimisation, indexation monitoring and schema validation
  • Internal linking to consolidate authority across topic clusters and support priority service pages
