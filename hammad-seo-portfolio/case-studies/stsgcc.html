The outcome

A 1.5% CTR at an average position of 15.6 shows that the site earns clicks even from page two, which reflects strong titles and descriptions for a specialised B2B audience.

The next phase focuses on moving priority service queries from page two onto page one, where click-through rates are typically several times higher.

Strategy applied

  • Rewrote and restructured core service pages around commercial keywords for buyers of manpower supply, industrial trading, HSE and contracting services
  • Location-focused targeting for Al Jubail and the Eastern Province to match how local B2B buyers search
  • Content on common manpower and HSE questions, routing informational visitors toward service pages
  • Organization, Service and FAQ schema markup to strengthen entity recognition and featured snippet eligibility
  • Technical health monitoring: Core Web Vitals, indexation and crawl efficiency
  • Internal linking and monthly tracking focused on conversion-relevant queries
